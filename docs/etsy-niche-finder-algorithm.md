# Etsy Niche Finder — Browser Agent Procedure (Digital + Print on Demand)

Paste everything below this line into Claude in Chrome as the task.

---

## Mission

Find 3 to 5 niches on Etsy that are selling well RIGHT NOW and are still enterable by a new shop, across two tracks:

- Track A, Digital downloads: files the buyer downloads (planners, templates, SVG, PNG, clipart, wall art, worksheets).
- Track B, Print on demand (POD): designs printed on physical products by Printify (t-shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, mugs, tote bags, posters, tumblers, phone cases, ornaments, blankets, pillows).

The best outcome is a crossover niche: a design theme that sells as a digital file to crafters AND as a finished product to consumers. Flag those specially.

Work only on etsy.com. Do not use Pinterest, Google Trends, Printify, or third-party tools. Never buy, favorite, message, or sign in. Read only.

Definition of a winning niche: a specific buyer phrase (2 to 5 words) where
1. several page-1 listings are getting fresh reviews every week (demand is live), AND
2. several page-1 listings have few reviews or come from young shops (the door is open), AND
3. there is a visible quality gap you could beat, AND
4. for Track B only, page-1 buyers already accept POD products at a price that leaves margin over Printify base cost.

Keep a running scratch table in your working notes. Every keyword you touch gets a row. Update it as you go. At the end, output the tables described in Phase 6.

## Global rules

- Track A searches must have the Digital Downloads filter on. Set it once: search "planner", open Filters, set Item type to "Digital downloads" (label may read "Item format"), apply, copy the resulting URL. Reuse it for every Track A search by replacing the q= value.
- Track B searches use no item-type filter, or "Physical items" if the filter is present.
- Read the first page only (about 48 listings) unless told otherwise. Skip listings marked "Ad".
- Skip and flag any keyword containing a brand, character, celebrity, sports team, school, song lyric, movie quote, or franchise (Disney, Taylor Swift, Stanley, Barbie, NFL, Bluey, Marvel, Nike, Harry Potter, Friends, The Office and so on). This matters twice as much on Track B, where quotes and logos are the usual takedown reason. Blank-garment brand names like Bella Canvas, Gildan, or Comfort Colors are fine, they are the shirts, not the design.
- Reviews are the sales proxy. Rule of thumb: 1 review is roughly 8 to 15 sales.
- Today's date matters for Track B. Note the current month. Occasion niches (Halloween, Christmas, Mother's Day, graduation, back to school) show high review velocity only in their window. Record the season for every Track B phrase so a September win is not mistaken for a year-round win.
- Do not exceed the caps in each phase. Converging matters more than being exhaustive.
- If a page fails to load or Etsy shows a bot check, wait 10 seconds, retry once, then move to the next keyword and note the skip. Do not attempt to solve any CAPTCHA.

## How to recognize a POD listing (Track B)

Count a listing as POD when two or more of these are true:

- Photos are mockups: the same flat-lay or model photo style repeated across the shop with only the design changing.
- Listing text says "made to order", "printed when ordered", or names a production partner.
- Size chart or description names a blank: Bella Canvas 3001, Gildan 5000 or 18000, Comfort Colors 1717, Next Level, Stanley Stella.
- Processing time is 2 to 7 business days and the shop has dozens to hundreds of listings across the same 2 to 4 product types.
- Variants are color plus size grids with 10 or more color options.

## Printify base cost reference (approximate, USD, before shipping)

Use these for margin math. They drift, so treat them as estimates.

| product | base cost | typical shipping to US |
|---|---|---|
| t-shirt (Bella Canvas 3001, Gildan 5000) | 9 to 13 | 4 to 5 |
| sweatshirt (Gildan 18000) | 17 to 22 | 6 to 8 |
| hoodie (Gildan 18500) | 20 to 26 | 6 to 8 |
| mug 11oz | 5 to 8 | 5 to 6 |
| tote bag | 8 to 11 | 4 to 5 |
| poster or print | 5 to 15 | 5 to 8 |
| tumbler 20oz | 14 to 20 | 6 to 8 |
| phone case | 9 to 13 | 4 to 5 |
| ornament | 6 to 10 | 4 to 5 |
| blanket | 25 to 40 | 8 to 12 |

Etsy takes roughly 10 percent in fees, plus 12 to 15 percent more on orders that come through Offsite Ads. A listing needs to sell at about 2x base cost plus shipping to be worth doing.

## Phase 0: Set up the scratch table

Columns for the keyword table:

| keyword | track | level | source | product_type | season | results_shown | p1_bestseller_count | p1_listings_100plus | p1_listings_under_30 | p1_min_reviews | p1_distinct_shops | p1_pod_count | price_low | price_median | price_high | new_sort_hits | crossover_partner | status | notes |

product_type, p1_pod_count, price_median, and crossover_partner are used mainly on Track B. Leave blank where not applicable.

Status values: screened, expand, dig, validate, WINNER, dead, trademark, skipped.

## Phase 1: Broad seeds (level 1)

### Track A seeds, cap 8 roots

planner, template, printable, wall art, invitation, worksheet, journal, tracker, resume, spreadsheet, clipart, png, svg, sticker, coloring page, flashcards, checklist, workbook, pattern, font, mockup, preset, procreate, notion, canva, goodnotes, sublimation, cricut, logo, menu, sign, label, calendar, budget, meal plan, lesson plan, certificate

Also harvest roots from Etsy's own structure: open the homepage, hover the main categories, and note any subcategory whose name implies digital (Digital Prints, Digital Art, Templates, Printables, Fonts, Clip Art, Patterns). Add up to 4 of those.

### Track B seeds, cap 8 roots, built as theme plus product

Pick 4 product types from: shirt, sweatshirt, hoodie, mug, tote bag, poster, tumbler, ornament, phone case.

Pick 6 themes from: nurse, teacher, dog mom, cat mom, mama, grandma, dad, retirement, birthday, bachelorette, bridesmaid, funny, sarcastic, mental health, gym, fishing, hunting, camping, book lover, coffee, plant lady, gardening, nursing student, graduation, Christmas, Halloween, fall, western, cottagecore, retro, vintage, Bible verse, motivational, trendy, aesthetic, gift for him, gift for her.

Form roots as theme plus product, for example "nurse sweatshirt", "funny mug", "dog mom shirt", "Halloween sweatshirt". Cap 8.

Record every root as a level-1 row. Do not score yet. Note the "Related searches" chips on each results page for Phase 2.

## Phase 2: Expand each root into buyer phrases (level 2) — cap 12 phrases per root

For each root, collect phrases from three places:

1. Search bar autocomplete. Type the root plus a space, record every suggestion. Then type the root plus a space plus each of these letters and record suggestions: a, b, c, d, f, g, h, k, m, n, p, r, s, t, w. Stop early at 12.
2. Related searches chips on the results page.
3. Title patterns. Read the titles of the first 12 listings. Extract recurring modifier words and group them into: audience (nurse, teacher, ADHD, mom, realtor), format (Canva, Goodnotes, PDF, PNG, SVG, Notion, Excel), style (boho, minimalist, vintage, watercolor, retro, groovy), occasion (wedding, Christmas, back to school), and use (weekly, budget, meal, habit, gift). Combine root plus one modifier to form phrases not already found.

Dedupe. Drop anything with a brand. Add each phrase as a level-2 row.

Hard cap for the whole run: 80 level-2 phrases on Track A, 60 on Track B. If you hit it, stop expanding and move on.

## Phase 3: Quick screen every level-2 phrase

For each phrase, open the search (Track A: digital filter on; Track B: no filter), sort by relevancy, and record from page 1, ignoring Ads:

- results_shown: the result count if Etsy displays one, else "n/a"
- p1_bestseller_count: listings with a "Bestseller" or "Popular now" badge
- p1_listings_100plus: listings with 100 or more reviews
- p1_listings_under_30: listings with fewer than 30 reviews
- p1_min_reviews: the smallest review count on the page
- p1_distinct_shops: how many different shop names appear
- price_low, price_median, price_high: from non-Ad listings. On Track B use the displayed price, which is usually the cheapest variant
- Track B only, p1_pod_count: how many page-1 listings meet the POD test above
- Track B only, season: year-round, or the occasion window if the phrase is occasion-bound

Then change sort to "Most recent" and count new_sort_hits: among the first 24 newest listings, how many already have 5 or more reviews. This measures whether newcomers are selling.

Screen decision, apply in order:

- dead if p1_listings_100plus is 0 and p1_bestseller_count is 0. Nobody is buying.
- dead if p1_listings_under_30 is 0 and p1_min_reviews is above 200. Locked up by incumbents.
- dead if p1_distinct_shops is 3 or fewer. One or two shops own it.
- Track B only, dead if p1_pod_count is under 6. Buyers here want handmade, embroidered, or engraved, and a POD listing will look cheap next to them.
- Track B only, dead if price_median is under 2x the base cost of that product type. No margin.
- dig if p1_listings_100plus is 3 or more AND p1_listings_under_30 is 3 or more. Demand plus open door.
- dig if new_sort_hits is 3 or more. Newcomers are winning, whatever else is true.
- expand if p1_listings_100plus is 6 or more and p1_listings_under_30 is 0 to 2. Demand is real but the phrase is too broad. Send it back through Phase 2 as a root one level deeper, cap 6 phrases.
- otherwise screened, no action.

Cap: dig on at most 10 phrases per track. If more qualify, rank by new_sort_hits, then p1_listings_under_30, then p1_listings_100plus, and take the top 10.

## Phase 4: Dig (level 3) — go one level more specific

For each dig phrase, repeat Phase 2 with a cap of 6 child phrases, then screen the children with Phase 3.

Stop digging a branch when either:
- a child gets p1_listings_100plus of 0 to 1. Too specific, demand vanished. Keep the parent.
- the child scores dig again. Keep the child, it is sharper than the parent.

Never go past level 4. A niche should be nameable in 5 words or fewer.

Collect every phrase at any level whose status is dig into the validation set. Cap 6 per track. Rank the same way as in Phase 3.

## Phase 4b: Crossover check

Every design theme can be sold two ways. Before validation, run one extra search per candidate to find its partner on the other track:

- For each Track A candidate that is an SVG, PNG, clipart, or sublimation design, strip the format word and search the theme plus "shirt" and the theme plus "sweatshirt" with no filter. Record the best partner phrase and its p1_listings_100plus and p1_pod_count in crossover_partner.
- For each Track B candidate, strip the product word and search the theme plus "svg" and the theme plus "png" with the digital filter. Record the best partner phrase and its p1_listings_100plus in crossover_partner.

A partner counts as live when it has 3 or more page-1 listings with 100+ reviews. Crossover candidates get a bonus in scoring and are reported first, because one design file feeds two listings: a digital download for crafters and a Printify product for everyone else.

## Phase 5: Validate each candidate niche

For each candidate, open the top 5 non-Ad listings by relevancy and, for each, record:

- listing review count
- reviews in the last 30 days: open the reviews on the listing page, sorted newest first, count how many carry a date within the last 30 days, stop at 30
- price, and on Track B the price of the most common variant, since the displayed price is usually the cheapest
- "in X carts" or "X people bought this in the past 24 hours" if shown
- shop total sales, shown on the shop page header
- shop age: "On Etsy since" on the shop's About section or the shop page
- shop listing count
- Track B only: processing time, ships-from country, number of color variants, and whether the listing is POD by the test above

Then read the 1, 2, and 3 star reviews on the top 2 listings and write one sentence on the recurring complaint. On Track A this is usually missing sizes, unclear instructions, hard to edit, ugly fonts. On Track B it is usually print faded or cracked, sizing runs small, color not as pictured, slow shipping, design smaller than expected. Note which complaints you can fix by choosing a better Printify provider, better mockups, or a clearer size guide, versus which are inherent to POD.

Also open the shop of the youngest shop on page 1 and note its age, sales, and listing count. A shop under 12 months old with 500 or more sales is the strongest signal that the niche is enterable today.

Score each candidate:

- Demand velocity (0 to 5): sum of reviews in the last 30 days across the top 5 listings. 0 to 4 = 0, 5 to 14 = 1, 15 to 29 = 2, 30 to 59 = 3, 60 to 99 = 4, 100 or more = 5.
- Open door (0 to 5): number of page-1 listings that are under 30 reviews OR from a shop under 12 months old. 0 = 0, 1 to 2 = 1, 3 to 4 = 2, 5 to 7 = 3, 8 to 11 = 4, 12 or more = 5.
- Price room (0 to 3). Track A: 0 if price_high is under 3 dollars, 1 if under 6, 2 if under 12, 3 if 12 or more. Track B: compute margin = price_median minus base cost minus shipping minus 10 percent of price_median. 0 if margin under 4 dollars, 1 if under 8, 2 if under 12, 3 if 12 or more.
- Quality gap (0 to 2): 0 if reviews show no consistent complaint, 1 if one clear complaint, 2 if the same complaint appears on multiple top listings.
- Crossover bonus (0 to 2): 0 if no live partner, 1 if the partner has 3 to 5 page-1 listings with 100+ reviews, 2 if 6 or more.
- Track B seasonality penalty: subtract 2 if the phrase is occasion-bound and the occasion is more than 8 weeks away or already passed. Subtract 0 if it is year-round or the window is open now.

Total is out of 17. WINNER if total is 10 or more AND demand velocity is 2 or more AND open door is 2 or more. Otherwise mark validate-failed with the reason.

If fewer than 3 winners come out across both tracks, go back to Phase 4 and take the next best candidates, up to one extra pass.

## Phase 6: Output

Output four things, in this order:

1. Crossover winners first, then the rest. One row per WINNER:

| niche phrase | track | level | season | demand_velocity | open_door | price_room | quality_gap | crossover | total | typical price | est. margin per sale (Track B) | example top listing (title only) | youngest strong shop (age, sales) | main complaint to beat | suggested first product |

For "suggested first product" on a crossover niche, name both halves: the digital file to list and the Printify product to link it to.

2. Near misses: candidates that scored 8 or 9, with the single reason they fell short. Include any Track B phrase that failed only on seasonality, with the date its window opens.

3. Trademark flags: every phrase skipped for brand or quote risk, so they are not re-researched by mistake.

4. The full keyword table as CSV in a code block, so the run can be resumed or audited.

Finish with a 6-line summary: how many roots, phrases, digs, and validations were done per track, which winner you would start with and why, and which product types on Track B showed the most POD acceptance.
